Privacy Policy

TabSweep for Gmail · last updated 22 October 2026

TabSweep for Gmail ("TabSweep") is a browser extension made by Sercan Erhan. It adds a button to Gmail that marks unread conversations as read or archives them.

What TabSweep does with your mail

Nothing leaves your browser. TabSweep does not read the content of your emails, does not store them, and does not send them anywhere. It only looks at the page structure of mail.google.com to count unread rows and to press Gmail's own "Select → Unread", "Mark as read" and "Archive" controls on your behalf. TabSweep does not use the Gmail API and never asks for access to your Google account.

What TabSweep stores

  • Your preferences (for example "ask before archiving") are stored in your browser's extension storage and, if you are signed in to your browser, synced by the browser the way it syncs any extension setting.
  • License state (whether a trial has started, or a purchase exists) is cached in local extension storage for up to one hour.

Licensing and payments

Trials and purchases are handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for payments. When you start a trial or buy a license, ExtensionPay receives the email address you enter on its page and a random installation id generated by the extension. TabSweep never sees your card details. ExtensionPay's privacy policy applies to that data: extensionpay.com/privacy.

What TabSweep does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers.
  • No access to any site other than mail.google.com and extensionpay.com.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
