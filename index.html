TabSweep for Gmail

One click clears the unread mail in any Gmail tab.

TabSweep adds a button to Gmail's own toolbar. Click it and every unread conversation on the tab you're looking at is marked as read. The menu next to it archives them instead, or sweeps the whole tab beyond the page you see.

  • Works on Primary, Promotions, Social, Updates, labels and searches
  • Personal Gmail and Google Workspace, any number of accounts
  • No access to your mail content: it presses Gmail's buttons for you
  • Free for 7 days, then a small yearly or one-time price
